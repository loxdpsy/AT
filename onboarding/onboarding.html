Choose how pages and selected text are translated. You can change this later in Options.

1 Translation provider

Pick one. The recommended choice runs on your computer and sends no text to a web service.

Groq and OpenRouter need your own key. Add it in Options, Services, after you save.

2 Connect the Atlas Helper (for Offline)

The helper is a small app for this computer. It runs the offline translation, keeps the language packs, and stays in the tray. The browser cannot install it for you, so you download and open it once.

Checking the helper...
Download the helper

  1. On the releases page, download atlas-updater-windows-setup.exe and run it. Choose the folder, or keep the default. It adds a Start menu entry and needs no administrator password.
  2. Press Connect this browser here. The helper window asks you; choose Allow.
  3. In the helper's Offline tab, install the language pairs you need. Each pair is about 37 MB.
