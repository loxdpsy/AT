                DeepL

                Uses the official DeepL API. Free keys end with :fx and are routed to api-free.deepl.com automatically; Pro keys use api.deepl.com.

                Groq (AI)

                An AI model that translates whole sections in context. Add your own key from console.groq.com. The text you translate is sent to Groq with that key.

                LibreTranslate

                Text translation through your own LibreTranslate server.
